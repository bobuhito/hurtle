Cairo Tiler

1. The Tile

All tiles are identical Cairo pentagons consisting of four sides of length 1 and one short side of length √3 − 1 (≈ 0.732), with three 120° interior angles and two 90° right angles.

2. Tile Placement

  • Players alternate turns placing one tile at a time.
  • Edge Alignment: Every new tile must share at least one full edge with a previously placed tile.
  • Vertex Matching: Abutting edges must match vertex angles (90° corners align with 90° corners, and 120° corners align with 120° corners).
  • No Overlap: Tiles abut along edges; tile interiors must never overlap.

3. Isolated Regions & Winning Condition

  • Isolated Region: An un-tiled area fully enclosed by tiles. Boundaries are sealed by abutting edges, as well as corner-to-corner ("kitty-corner") contact.
  • Continued Play: An isolated region does not end the game immediately. Players may continue placing tiles inside any isolated region on subsequent turns.
  • Game End & Winner: The game ends as soon as an isolated region becomes fully completed (no further legal tile placements are possible inside it). The player who places the tile that completes the region wins immediately.
